# Product Overview

## Summary

Seeds is a browser-based creative sparring tool for turning briefs into structured possibility.

It helps strategists, designers, and idea-makers move from a blank slate or early thought into a sharpened set of strategic and creative directions. Instead of generating a finished strategy deck in one pass, Seeds generates multiple short atomic idea cards arranged across an evolving creative canvas. The user then selects, edits, adds, combines, locks, and riffs on those cards across a small number of rounds. The system uses those signals to re-synthesize the next round around the user's emerging intent.

The result is not a prematurely polished deck. The result is a coherent, structured strategic output that can be exported as markdown and used downstream for presentation-building or further creative development.

## Product thesis

Most AI creative tools fail by trying to jump straight from brief to polished answer. That skips the collaborative middle, which is where strong creative and strategic work actually gets made.

Seeds is built around a different model:

1. AI generates structured possibility.
2. The human curates and shapes meaning.
3. The system recomposes around those choices.
4. Multiple rounds move from breadth to coherence.
5. Final output becomes a structured handoff rather than a one-shot presentation.

This makes Seeds a co-creation system rather than an auto-deck machine.

## Who it is for

Seeds is designed for creative professionals who need help getting from ambiguity to a promising strategic shape.

Primary users in v1:
- strategists
- designers
- creative directors
- idea-makers

Seeds is designed first for a single user, not for a live team workflow. Collaboration can be added later, but v1 should assume one person driving the canvas.

## Core job-to-be-done

Take a brief and present a series of considered paths to walk down, then refine those paths into a sharpened diamond.

Supporting jobs:
- turn a blank-page moment into multiple starting points
- provide breadth without chaos
- allow the user to hybridize elements from different directions
- help the user progressively sharpen taste and direction
- produce a clean markdown handoff for downstream outputs

## What Seeds is

Seeds is:
- a standalone app
- a pre-slides ideation and shaping environment
- a structured visual canvas
- an AI-assisted but human-led creative workflow
- a system built around short atomic cards rather than long documents
- a multi-round refinement experience
- a tool for surfacing and shaping directions, not finalizing them too early

## What Seeds is not

Seeds is not:
- a one-shot deck generator
- a chat-first interface
- a client presentation tool
- a heavy project management system
- a corpus-dependent retrieval product
- a universal collaboration suite in v1
- a tool that forces the user through rigid approval steps before ideation starts

## Product promise

Seeds should make the user feel like they are working with a creative sparring partner that is fast, generative, and structurally useful without becoming overbearing.

It should help them:
- see more directions faster
- discover combinations they may not have reached alone
- avoid generic one-shot AI outputs
- retain authorship over the work
- exit with a sharper strategic shape than they started with

## Core interaction loop

1. The user enters a prompt and/or uploads brief files.
2. Seeds generates an initial round of idea cards across a loose creative flow.
3. The user selects, edits, adds, notes, locks, drags, and riffs on cards.
4. Seeds generates a new round based on those choices.
5. This repeats for roughly three to four rounds.
6. The user exports a consolidated markdown output.

## Why the card model matters

The card is the core object in Seeds because it allows the system to work at the level of creative ingredients rather than finished arguments.

Cards should feel like short thought fragments, not mini decks. They are designed to be:
- fast to scan
- easy to combine
- easy to reject
- easy to rewrite
- useful as signal for the next round

This atomic structure is what allows Seeds to behave like a real co-creation tool instead of a long-form AI writer.

## Why rounds matter

Rounds are how the product turns interaction into meaning.

Each round should feel like a fresh canvas informed by the previous one. A round is not a simple reroll. It is a new synthesis that reflects:
- what the user selected
- what they edited
- what they wrote themselves
- what they locked in place
- what they chose to riff on

Over time, rounds should move from divergence to convergence.

## Output model

The output of Seeds in v1 should be a crisp markdown artifact rather than a polished deck export.

This output should capture:
- the original brief context
- the chosen strategic direction
- retained core ingredients
- supporting ideas and modifiers
- user-created additions
- alternatives or appendix content
- a strong downstream prompt for presentation or concept generation

## Product ambition

Seeds should feel like the beginning of a new category of creative software: a structured thinking canvas where AI expands possibility and humans shape direction.

Its ambition is not to mimic presentation tools or chatbots. Its ambition is to create a better environment for the messy but high-value middle of strategy and idea development.
