# Test Briefs

## Purpose

Provide a small set of repeatable briefs for evaluating Seeds behavior.

## Brief 1: Fashion launch

Prompt:
- `Help me develop a strategy and creative concept directions for an Adidas x Wales Bonner launch in Paris.`

What it tests:
- style sensitivity
- cultural signals
- campaign idea generation

## Brief 2: B2B product repositioning

Prompt:
- `We need strategic territory and launch ideas for repositioning a workflow automation platform from efficiency tool to team intelligence layer.`

What it tests:
- abstract strategic framing
- non-consumer language handling

## Brief 3: Hospitality concept

Prompt:
- `Help shape brand direction and activation ideas for a new members club that mixes wellness, food, and late-night culture in Lisbon.`

What it tests:
- place-based imagination
- tone and audience nuance

## Brief 4: Social impact campaign

Prompt:
- `Develop direction territories and creative sparks for a campaign that makes repair culture feel aspirational to Gen Z.`

What it tests:
- value-led messaging
- generational framing without cliche

## Recommended v1 decision

For each test brief, run:
- one Round 1 generation
- one local riff on a promising card
- one Round 2 growth after meaningful edits and locks
- one markdown export

## Evaluation note

Use the same test briefs across prompt revisions so quality changes are comparable.
