# Round Improvement Checklist

## Purpose

Give a practical checklist for reviewing whether a new round is better than the prior one.

## Checklist

- Does the round clearly reflect the user's selected cards?
- Do locked cards remain present or meaningfully represented?
- Are user-authored and edited cards shaping the language of the new board?
- Has noise been reduced without losing useful breadth?
- Are the columns still legible and useful?
- Are the cards distinct from one another?
- Is there at least one compelling direction worth pursuing further?
- Does the round feel more coherent than the previous one?
- If ambiguity remains, is it productive rather than sloppy?

## Recommended v1 decision

Review this checklist for:
- prompt revisions
- major model changes
- regressions reported from real usage

## Use in development

- apply during manual QA
- use as annotation guidance for eval datasets
- use before accepting major generation logic changes
