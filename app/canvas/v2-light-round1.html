Nuggets 6
Grace Wales Bonner described her work as "an Afro-Atlantic mythology" — this isn't sportswear, it's cultural infrastructure.
#heritage
Paris is the only city where sportswear and haute couture share the same sidewalk without irony.
#location
The Samba silhouette is already Paris coded. Don't launch a new shoe — recontextualize what already belongs.
#product
WB's color palette — ochre, cream, terracotta — is already Parisian. The collection was made for this city.
#visual
The 3-stripe motif as crochet, embroidery, or textile pattern — not a logo but a handcraft gesture.
#craft
Directions 5
"Not a drop, a homecoming." Position the Paris launch as WB's cultural return, not a product release.
#core
Make Paris the co-creator. Source materials, venues, and talent exclusively from Paris's African diaspora communities.
#activation
No pop-up store. An open studio — visitors see pieces being finished, altered, customized in real time.
#experience
The campaign should feel found, not made. No studio shoots. Real Parisians wearing pieces in their own context.
#content
Price transparency as a cultural stance — show the markup is going back to the communities that inspired it.
#pricing
Ideas 6
"Samba Parlour" — a barbershop-meets-atelier where you get a fresh cut and first access to the collection.
#activation
Commission Parisian musicians to create a sonic identity — pressing it on vinyl as the launch collectible.
#music
Archive exhibition: display every WB × Adidas piece ever made in chronological order. Let the legacy sell itself.
#brand
A "Paris edition" colorway available only in-person, never online. Create a pilgrimage mechanic.
#scarcity
Modifiers 5
Constraint: no celebrity seeding. Let the clothes move through real communities, not parasocial relationships.
#rule
Tone: quiet confidence. Think Lemaire catalogue, not Supreme drop. Whisper, don't shout.
#tone
Risk: Paris can feel like a cliché. Avoid Eiffel Tower adjacent anything. Go 10e, 18e, 20e — not 1er.
#risk
Every touchpoint should feel handmade — even if it's at scale. Imperfection as intentional craft signal.
