From blank page to
sharp direction

Seeds works in rounds. Each one responds to your judgement, so the work gets sharper as you go.

1

Plant a seed

Start with a rough prompt, a brief, or a few source files. You don't need to know the answer yet.

2

Shape the board

Seeds generates a canvas of atomic cards: tensions, signals, directions, ideas. Keep what matters, reject what doesn't.

3

Grow the next round

Your selections become signals for the next generation. The work moves from breadth to coherence, naturally.

4

Riff locally

Push on any single card without regenerating the whole board. Follow a thread without losing the bigger picture.

5

Find the direction

After a few rounds, the noise clears. You're left with a direction you believe in because you shaped it.

6

Export and hand off

When it's ready, export a clean markdown brief. A structured artifact, not an ephemeral conversation.

A different kind of AI tool

Most AI tools give you a wall of text or jump to a polished answer. That skips the part where good work actually gets made.

Canvas, not chat

Ideas live as small, movable cards on a spatial board. Not buried in paragraphs of prose you'll never re-read.

Works in rounds

Each generation responds to your selections. The system learns what you care about and pushes further in that direction.

Widens before it narrows

AI should open up possibility, not close it down. Seeds explores multiple directions before converging on one.

You keep authorship

Taste is revealed through selection and editing. The best ideas come from your judgement shaping the machine's output.
