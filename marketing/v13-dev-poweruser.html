AI canvas for
creative strategy

type: web-app · status: open-access · stack: next.js + reactflow + supabase

Brief your ambition. AI generates strategic seeds — tensions, signals, ideas, provocations — across thinking zones. Curate with taste. Distill to one clear direction.

// spec
inputbrief + modifiers (audience, constraints, references)
output12 strategic cards across 4 zones per round
card types tension signal direction idea provocation guard rail
actionsstar, reject, riff, distill, move, duplicate
zones4 default, user-configurable, resizable, colored borders
themes9 colorways (6 color + 3 mono), light/dark toggle
exportcanvas snapshot, card list, distilled brief

// card actions
star
Mark a card as promising — carries forward to distilled zone
reject
Remove from canvas — card fades out with scale animation
riff
Generate variations on a single card — explore adjacent ideas
distill
Promote to distilled zone — builds the final direction
duplicate
Copy card to same or different zone
move
Drag between zones — reorganize as you think
