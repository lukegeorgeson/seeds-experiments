## Design Context

### Users
Strategists, designers, and idea-makers who need to move from a blank brief into sharpened strategic and creative directions. They're experienced professionals who value clarity over flash. They use this tool during focused creative thinking sessions — the interface should disappear and let the thinking breathe.

### Brand Personality
Calm, sharp, premium. A creative thinking tool, not a playful whiteboard or noisy AI console.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Direction:** Editorial/Refined — spacious, restrained, precise, warm enough to invite exploration
- **Decoration:** Intentional — subtle texture and spatial depth, never gratuitous
- **Anti-references:** FigJam's playful roundness, Miro's visual noise, generic SaaS dashboards, purple gradient AI slop
- **Positive references:** Are.na (content-first quietness), Cosmos (spatial contemplation), Linear (precision + restraint), Monocle (editorial confidence)
- **Theme:** Both light and dark modes, accent color `#eac1f5` (purple/pink)

### Design Principles
1. **Quiet confidence** — The interface earns trust through restraint. Every element justifies its presence.
2. **Typography does the heavy lifting** — Hierarchy through type size, weight, and spacing — not decoration or color.
3. **Color is earned** — Monochrome by default. Color appears semantically (card types, zone accents) and feels intentional, never decorative.
4. **Spatial generosity** — Generous whitespace invites thinking. Dense layouts signal productivity tools; spacious layouts signal thinking tools.
5. **No AI slop** — No purple gradients, no uniform bubbly border-radius, no centered-everything layouts. Every design choice should feel like a human designer made it with conviction.
